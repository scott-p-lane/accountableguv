U.S. Net Income

These dashboards summarize the net income that U.S. government has. The data is taken directly from the Monthly Treasury Statements (MTS) that are published monthly by the U.S. Department of the Treasury. The dashboards use "Receipts of the U.S. Government" (Table 4). Table 4 allows us to review both aggregate incoming funds as well as their sources.

Net Income: Yearly Comparisons

Compare federal net receipts across fiscal years using the dashboard's interactive controls. Though the site is using the data from the treasury, when presenting it, we round the values (to the nearest billion) for clarity. Check its coverage dates before comparing incomplete periods. If the dashboard does not load or is difficult to use on your device, open it directly.When comparing current fiscal year with prior, ensure you are comparing complete periods. In other words, don't compare months in prior years that are not yet present in the current fiscal year data.

Quarterly and monthly views are planned for this page.
