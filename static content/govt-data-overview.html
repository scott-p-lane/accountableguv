Monthly Treasury Statement

The Monthly Treasury Statement (MTS), prepared by the U.S. Treasury's Bureau of the Fiscal Service, summarizes the federal government's receipts, outlays, and resulting budget deficit or surplus. It also reports financing activity and trust fund information, providing context for how money flows through government.

How often is it published?

Treasury publishes the MTS monthly, normally on the eighth workday of the month following the reporting month. Each release reports the preceding month's budget activity, with fiscal-year-to-date totals and comparisons where applicable. See Treasury's publication timing guidance.

What can you explore?

The nine numbered MTS tables covered by this site describe budget results and estimates, receipts by source, outlays by agency and function, financing, monthly trends, and trust fund activity.
