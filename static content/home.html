Accountability Starts with Understanding the Impact of Government Spending

We connect public, trusted data sources tracking government finances and correlate them with legislation, policy, and government operations to make the results easier to examine, discuss, and improve.

By understanding the flow of funds, we can better assess the impact of government decisions, and that is where accountability begins.

Our mission

Understand the impact of government spending. Drive accountabiltiy from clear, accessible insights who is impacted and how via trusted, public data.

At the end of the day, accountability starts with making it easy for anyone to see how public funds are collected and spent. That's the easy part. Now we need to make it easier to understand who is impacted and how by those funds. That's tougher, especially when we must do so free of political bias.

01 / ANALYZE

Follow the Money!

Bring together trustworthy public records and datasets to understand the flow of government funds.

Explore the Net Income dashboard to follow federal net receipts using the U.S. Treasury's Monthly Treasury Statement (MTS).
02 / CONNECT

Capture the Impact

Relate legislation and policy choices to the agencies, programs, and operations they shape. Use their own (public) data to illustrate the real-world effects.

03 / DISCUSS

Build shared context

Present findings clearly so people can debate the evidence and possible changes constructively.
