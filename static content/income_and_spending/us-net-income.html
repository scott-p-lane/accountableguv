Notes and observations

Reading the data

  • Compare complete, equivalent periods. Do not compare a partial current fiscal year against a full prior fiscal year.
  • Check the dashboard's coverage dates and active filters before drawing conclusions.
  • Displayed values are rounded to the nearest billion for clarity.
  • Table 4 separates gross receipts, refunds, and net receipts. Net receipts describe incoming funds after refunds, rather than a budget surplus or deficit.

Data sources and processing

This dashboard uses MTS Table 4: Receipts of the U.S. Government, published by the U.S. Department of the Treasury. The source includes receipt categories, refunds, and net receipts for the current month, fiscal year to date, and comparable prior fiscal year to date.
